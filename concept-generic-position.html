Generic position

Generic position exists because special coincidences are too brittle to reason from. If three things meet at exactly one point, or a curve just kisses another curve, a tiny nudge can change the picture. The course wants arguments that survive tiny nudges, so it first moves the picture away from accidental perfection.

Concept Essay

Generic position is the course's way of refusing to build a proof on a coincidence. If two curves merely touch, if three intersections happen at exactly one point, or if an equilibrium is smeared into a whole line, the picture may be too delicate to reveal the stable reason. A tiny nudge could change it. The ordinary case is the case that survives small disturbances and therefore can be counted cleanly.

The first-principles need is evidence that does not vanish when the hand drawing is slightly adjusted. A curve that just kisses another curve may separate after the smallest motion. A clean crossing remains a recognizable local event. A whole line of equilibria may split into isolated defects after a small change, and those isolated defects can be inspected one at a time. Generic position asks the proof to work with those inspectable events.

Many of Tokieda's arguments need events that can be separated and inspected: isolated intersections, pairs being born or canceled, and defects that can be assigned an index. Generic position turns a crowded or coincidental picture into a ledger of small readable events. After the stable case is understood, exceptional cases can be read as limits or transition moments. The tempting mistake is to think the exceptional picture is more honest because it looks more exact. Often it is less honest, because it hides the mechanism that persists under small motion.

From first principles, the idea is a promise about evidence. If a proof counts meetings, then the meetings should be separate enough to count. If a proof assigns a sign, then the local picture should be clear enough for plus or minus to mean something. Generic position prepares the object so the later invariant is not built on an accident.

This does not mean the course ignores special cases. It means the proof first studies the stable nearby situation where the mechanism can be seen. Then the special case can be approached as a limiting or transition case. The clean picture is not a simplification of the theorem; it is the microscope that lets the theorem's evidence appear.

First Principles

Imagine trying to understand a room while every chair is exactly lined up with every table edge. That neatness is a trap: tiny movements destroy it. Generic position means shifting the picture just enough that fragile coincidences are gone. Then crossings happen one at a time, contacts are clean, and the argument sees the structure rather than the accident.

Important Detail

The shift must be small enough that it does not change the real question. It removes accidental equalities, not the object being studied.

Principle Behind It

Stable reasoning begins with a nearby case that survives small disturbances. The small move removes accidental coincidences while keeping the object and the question fixed.

Failure Mode

Ordinary position can sound like avoiding difficult cases. The course uses it differently: solve the stable case first, then read exceptional cases as limits or controlled moments where stable pictures change without destroying the question. The special case is explained by the nearby ordinary cases, not ignored.

Course Role

This idea is quiet but constant. It is behind clean intersections, pair creation and cancellation, isolated equilibria, and the move from messy physical examples to countable mathematical evidence. It gives the course permission to draw simple pictures without lying about the original problem, because the picture has first been moved away from accidents that would not survive a small nudge.

Anchor Example

Course moment: In the intersection lectures, a messy meeting is first nudged into clean separate meetings. That small cleanup is not cosmetic. It is what lets each meeting receive a sign or be paired with another meeting.
