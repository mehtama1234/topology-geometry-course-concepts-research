Lecture Reconstruction Drills

This page turns each lecture into a rebuild task. The reader starts from one concrete course moment and reconstructs the object, legal move, surviving fact, later use, and source check in order.

How To Use A Drill

Do the steps before rereading the lecture page. The order matters: start with the concrete object, state the permitted move, protect the surviving fact, connect to a second example, then attach the source family carefully. This prevents a lecture from shrinking into a term or theorem name.

The self-check and common-failure fields are the tests. If the reconstruction skips the legal move, ignores the protected fact, or cites a source before the course evidence is clear, the answer is not ready.
