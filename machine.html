Geometry → Gravity: One Machine

Differential geometry is not a prerequisite you leave behind — it is general relativity, one level up. The shapes, curvature, and invariants of the geometry course become the spacetime, gravity, and physics of the relativity course. Every GR idea below is a geometry idea applied to the fabric of spacetime.

Geometry & topology

General relativity

Spacetime as a manifold, geodesics as free fall, curvature as gravity, horizons and cosmic expansion — the language applied.
