How To Read This Audit

The counts below are not the goal by themselves. They are evidence that the site has enough places where a reader can check the real standard: object before term, legal move before conclusion, protected fact before theorem name, and caveat before source claim. If a later page becomes shallow, this audit should point to the layer that needs repair.
