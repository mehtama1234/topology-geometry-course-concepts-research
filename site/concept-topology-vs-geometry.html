Topology and geometry

Geometry cares about measured shape: distance, angle, area, curvature. Topology cares about connectedness, holes, boundary, and what survives bending. The course needs both because some questions depend on exact bending while others depend only on the routes and obstructions inside the shape.

Important Detail

The distinction is not a wall. Geometry often produces the local measurements, while topology explains why their total has no freedom.

Principle Behind It

Choose the level of description that keeps the real constraint and discards the distracting details.
