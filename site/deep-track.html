The Deep Track

15 first-principles deep dives that teach topology by computing it. Each starts from a plain puzzle, builds the idea in everyday words, and shows it on a small program we actually ran — counting corners and edges, summing the swirls of a flow, integrating curvature, counting a knot's colorings — with computed numbers, an honest limit, and where the idea reappears. The thread throughout: a discrete count that equals a continuous invariant nothing can bend away.
